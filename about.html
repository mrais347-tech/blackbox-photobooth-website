Our story

Every celebration deserves to be remembered.

At BlackBox Photobooth, we believe every celebration deserves to be remembered. We provide a premium photobooth experience that captures genuine smiles, laughter, and unforgettable moments at weddings, corporate events, birthdays, graduations, and private celebrations.

Based in Subang Jaya, we proudly serve Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, offering unlimited high-quality instant prints, fully customised photo templates, fun props, and a friendly team dedicated to making your event memorable.

Our goal is simple: to create an interactive experience that brings people together while giving every guest a keepsake they'll cherish long after the event ends.

Whether you're planning an intimate gathering or a large-scale celebration, BlackBox Photobooth is here to turn every moment into a lasting memory.
